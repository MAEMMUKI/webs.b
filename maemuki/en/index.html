FAQ

What people ask us most

I don’t know anything about tech. Can I still do this?

Yes. That’s what we’re here for. We set everything up, explain it in person and stay available on WhatsApp for any question.

Will the AI talk weird to my customers?

No. The assistant answers with the information you give us (hours, menu, prices) in your business’s tone. If someone asks something it doesn’t know, it lets you know.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

We start with 3 months so you can see results. Then you decide: keep going, change plans or keep just the basics.

Why is there a monthly fee?

Because tech needs care: updating hours and prices, checking everything works, answering reviews and improving every month. That way you never end up with something abandoned.

Who owns my website and accounts?

You do. Your website, Google profile and social accounts stay in your business’s name.

Do you only work in New Jersey?

We start with businesses in New Jersey and New York so we can visit in person, and we also work remotely.
